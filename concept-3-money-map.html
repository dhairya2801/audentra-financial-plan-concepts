How your three biggest awards were figured

The formulas the offices actually use, with your numbers in them.

Federal Pell Grant
Max Pell 2026–27     $7,395− your SAI (from FAFSA)  $1,850= $5,545 / year → $2,772 this fall

SAI is the Student Aid Index the FAFSA computes from your family's income and size. Lower SAI, more Pell. Pell is yours as long as you stay enrolled at least half-time.

Florida Bright Futures · FAS
Rate per credit hour     $213× credits you're taking   15= $3,195 this fall

Paid per credit, so dropping to 12 credits lowers it to $2,556. Renews each year with a 3.0 GPA and 24 earned credits. Florida residents only.

Aster Merit Scholarship
Set at admission     $14,000 / yrApplies to         tuition only= $7,000 this fall

Aster's own money, so residency doesn't matter. Keep a 3.0 GPA and full-time enrollment to renew it for all four years.

The order Aster applies your money

Why the gap lands where it lands.

  1. Tuition-only awards first. Aster Merit and Bright Futures can't touch housing, so they go to tuition.
  2. Then flexible gift aid (Pell, Need Grant, outside scholarships) to tuition, fees, housing, meals, insurance, in that order.
  3. Then accepted loans. They count as "anticipated" credit on the bill even before they disburse.
  4. Then your family's payment. Whatever is left is the gap.
  5. Work-Study never touches the bill. It's paid to you every two weeks as you work, for living costs.
